# Analysis

Two to six plain sentences in our own words. Cover what you can support:

- The hook and its basis: inferred from the post, or seen in the first five seconds.
- The counts with their scope and date, and the like rate against the library median.
- The length and which timed structure it is closest to.
- Shot rhythm, when `scene_cuts` exist.
- For a raise: funding is the news, not proof the product works.

Do not write that a hook caused the views. Do not rate the product. No em dash, no en dash, no double hyphen.

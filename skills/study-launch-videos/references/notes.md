# Notes

Per entry: link, hook family, the beat timing in seconds, the proof unit (input, action, output, why it matters), and the evidence label.

Evidence labels come from the record: `hook_basis` and `kind_basis` say whether a label was inferred from the post or observed in the video. Snapshot `scope` says whether a count is per post or from a listing. Repeat both with every pattern.

Write each pattern in our own words, one or two lines. Never paste narration or post text beyond the stored excerpt. A record's analysis is a study note, not a transcript.
